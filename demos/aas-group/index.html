−0.30 How a patio is built

The bit you'll never see is the bit we're best at.

  1. Porcelain slabThe part you see, set to a gentle fall so rain runs off, not in.
  2. Mortar bedHolds every slab level and solid, with no hollow corners to crack.
  3. MOT Type 1 sub-baseCrushed stone, compacted. This is what carries the weight.
  4. Geotextile membraneKeeps the sub-base clean and stops weeds pushing up.
  5. Compacted subsoilDug down to firm ground, so nothing sinks later.

+0.00 Questions

Good questions to ask a landscaper.

Why do you start with a site visit?

Because every garden is different underneath. Ardian looks at the levels, the drainage and the ground before pricing anything, so your written quote matches the real job rather than a one-size estimate.

What do you do about drainage and levels?

We plan each project around the site's drainage, ground conditions and daily use, not just the finish on top: the right excavation depth, a properly compacted sub-base, a fall that takes water away, and edge restraints that stop paving creeping or cracking.

Which materials can I choose from?

Porcelain, natural stone, block paving, resin bound and concrete for hard surfaces; real turf or artificial grass; timber, composite or featheredge fencing on concrete posts; plus steps, edging, planting and lighting.

Which areas do you cover?

Northamptonshire (Northampton, Wellingborough, Kettering and Rothwell), Milton Keynes, Leicestershire (Market Harborough) and Southam and Coventry. Not sure if you're in? Put your postcode in the form and we'll confirm.

What is a Rootlok wall?

A living retaining wall. Tough geotextile bags are filled on site and locked together with interlocking plates, then seeded with grass and wildflowers that grow through. It holds a slope like a hard wall, without the concrete look, and needs no heavy machinery to install.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask Ardian at your free site visit. He'll talk you through what's covered for your job before any work starts.
