# kt-construction.co.uk audit (2026-11-04)

Screenshots: `current-1440*.png`, `current-390*.png`, `old-d.png`, `old-m.png`.

## Speed (Lighthouse mobile)
- Performance 52. LCP 46.4 s, FCP 5.9 s, Speed Index 7.0 s, TBT 310 ms.
- Total page weight 15.6 MB (full-size photos and PNG renders on the home page).

## What it does well
- Clear services, good SEO footprint (311 URLs: 99 colour pages, 74 guides, area and town pages), published prices, WhatsApp button, a 3-question style finder.

## Where it loses jobs
- Looks like most trade templates: carousel hero, stock-style layout. Nothing a designer or homeowner remembers.
- Many images are AI or manufacturer renders (Nova, Sahara, Velour, Lumea clinic, Ruboflex houses, Marmorino restaurant) mixed in with real jobs, which undermines the real work.
- Gallery captions assign towns to photos that look like stock or renders.
- The colour library (99 pages) isn't connected to a quote: you can't pick a colour for a room and send it.
- "From our Crawley base" vs a Hurstpierpoint address.
- Home says masterclasses are "coming soon" while `/workshops` lists prices.

## What the demo does instead
- Interactive hero that shows the core benefit (microcement over existing tiles, no grout).
- A 3D configurator wired to their real colour codes and published rates, which turns into a pre-filled brief.
- Real KT photos only, in a scroll-driven gallery.
- 2.2 s LCP on mobile.
