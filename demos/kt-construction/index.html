03Three millimetres

Everything happens in three millimetres.

0.0mm added
01 · Prepare

Your existing surface stays.

Sound tiles, concrete, plaster or plywood. We prepare the substrate properly first, because that is what makes the finish last.

02 · Base coats

Primer, then two base coats with mesh.

Reinforcing mesh goes into the base, which builds strength and flattens out the old surface, joints and all.

03 · Finishing coats

Two hand-trowelled finishing coats.

Fine coats, trowelled and burnished by hand. This is where the colour and the look come from, smooth or cloudy.

04 · Seal & protect

Tanked and sealed.

In wet rooms a tanking membrane goes on, then two coats of polyurethane sealer against dirt, moisture and daily wear. Waterproof once sealed.
