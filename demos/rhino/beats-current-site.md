# Where the new site beats rhinocoating.co.uk

These are plain-English points Dan can use in his reply to David.

1. **Visitors see a price before they share any details.** The new home page has a 30-second estimator: pick the space, the system (HB-200, FB-400 or M-Flow) and the size. It shows an indicative range and a monthly finance figure straight away. "Lock in a fixed quote" then fills in the quote form with those choices. The current site only offers a long form, so a visitor has to hand over their details before learning anything.

2. **The quote form is four short steps, and it takes the three photos he needs.** It asks for the space, then the postcode (with an instant "We cover Hale, you're in our home patch" check), then up to three floor photos with tips on what to shoot, then contact details. A thank-you page explains what happens next: survey, fixed quote, install date. WhatsApp is one tap away everywhere, including a sticky Call / WhatsApp / Quote bar on mobile.

3. **It shows why a Rhino floor lasts, rather than just saying so.** A scroll-driven 3D section takes his seven-layer system apart: concrete, crack repairs, diamond grinding, DPM, HB-200, flake and UV top coat, with one plain benefit per layer. It then locks back into a finished floor. The current home page uses iStock photos to show the process. The new site uses only his own job photos, and labels the one render as a render.

4. **It fixes errors that cost trust.** The current finance example says £98.12 × 24 = £2,904.88, but those payments actually come to £2,354.88. The new calculator works the figures properly (0% for 12 months, then 9% APR) and shows its working. The process is also explained one way throughout, where the current site says four stages in one place and six in another.

5. **Faster and better for search, with every ranking page kept.** All 32 existing URLs keep their exact paths, including the 10 city pages and 6 blog posts, so no rankings are thrown away. Each city page now has its own local angle: London basements, Glasgow cold-weather curing, Sheffield hillside access and so on. Every page has proper structured data for the business, services, FAQs and breadcrumbs.
