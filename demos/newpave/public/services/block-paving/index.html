Block paving, set out properly.

New concrete block or reclaimed brick, laid in the pattern you choose on a properly built base.

Book free survey 07787 707154

Patterns and colours

Herringbone is the strongest pattern for a drive you park on. Basketweave and stretcher bond suit paths, patios and smaller areas. You can mix colours, add a contrasting border or set in a feature.

How we lay it

  • Dig out to the right depth and lay a geotextile membrane.
  • Build up and compact an MOT Type 1 sub-base.
  • Screed a sharp sand bed, lay the blocks and cut in the edges.
  • Kiln-dried sand brushed into the joints and the whole drive compacted.

Steps and features

We build steps, kerbs and raised edges in matching blocks so the whole frontage ties together, like the curved front step and grey herringbone in our photos below.

Questions

Will weeds come through?

A membrane under the base and properly filled joints keep weeds to a minimum. Re-sanding the joints now and then keeps it that way.

Can you match my neighbour's blocks?

Often, yes. Show us on the survey and we will find the closest match.
