    What's under a driveway that lasts.

    Layer 1 of 5

    Dig out

    The old surface and any soft ground come out, down to a firm base. Levels and falls are set so water runs away from the house.

    Layer 2 of 5

    Geotextile membrane

    A tough fabric that stops the stone sinking into the soil and keeps weeds coming up from below.

    Layer 3 of 5

    MOT Type 1 sub-base

    Crushed stone, laid in layers and compacted. This is what carries the weight of a car for years.

    Layer 4 of 5

    Base course

    A bound base laid over the sub-base, so the resin has a solid, even surface to grip.

    Layer 5 of 5

    Resin-bound surface

    Stone and resin mixed on site and hand-trowelled smooth. Permeable, so rain soaks through instead of pooling.

    Depths depend on the ground and the traffic. Charles checks them on the free survey. [CONFIRM: build-up wording]

    Do I need planning permission for a new front drive?

    In England you don't need planning permission for a new or replacement front drive if it's permeable (like resin bound or permeable block paving), or if rainwater runs off onto a lawn or border in your garden.

    If a front drive over 5 m² is impermeable and drains onto the road, you need permission. This is the SuDS rule: sustainable drainage. Charles will tell you which applies to your drive on the survey.

    How long does a new drive take?

    It depends on the size, the surface and how much needs digging out. Your written quote will include the expected number of days. [CONFIRM: typical durations]

    How do I look after a resin or block drive?

    Sweep it regularly and give it an occasional wash. Clean up oil or fuel spills quickly. Keep heavy skips and sharp jacks off resin unless you protect it first. Block paving may need its joints topping up with kiln-dried sand over time.

    Can you do the dropped kerb too?

    A dropped kerb is on the public highway, so it needs approval from your local council (for example Telford & Wrekin, Shropshire, Wolverhampton or Birmingham City Council) before any work is done. Apply early, as it can take a few weeks. Charles can advise on how it fits with your new drive.

    Do you do commercial work?

    Yes. Newpave takes on domestic and commercial work, including car parks and shared drives.
