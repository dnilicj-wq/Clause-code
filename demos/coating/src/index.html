Why most garage floors peel (and why ours don't)

Most coating failures come down to three things: skipped prep, the wrong product and a rushed install. Big-box DIY kits sit on top of the concrete instead of bonding to it, so hot tires lift them right off.

Straight answers

How long does it last?

With proper prep and a polyaspartic system, decades of normal use. That's why prep is the step we never skip.

Will hot tires lift it?

Not with our system. Hot-tire pickup is a classic failure of thin DIY epoxy, which is exactly what we don't use.

Is it slippery?

Flake finishes add texture. We can add extra grip for wet areas.

What about cracks in my concrete?

We repair them as part of the prep.
