Built for Manchester weather.

What a properly laid drive does for you, every day.

Rain drains straight through.

Our resin drives are SUDS-compliant: rainwater soaks away through the surface instead of pooling or running into the road.

Herringbone takes the weight.

Blocks laid in herringbone lock together, so a car's weight spreads across the whole drive.

No weeds in the joins.

Resin is one seamless surface, with no joints for weeds to root in.
