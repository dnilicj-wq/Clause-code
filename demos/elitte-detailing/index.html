Why a studio

Not a van on a driveway. A studio.

Most detailers in the area work from a van. We work from a dedicated indoor studio with a hexagon LED ceiling and black slatted walls.

  1. Controlled light shows every defect

    The LED ceiling lights the paint evenly, so swirls and scratches can't hide. We see what needs doing, and so do you.

  2. No wind, rain or dust

    Nothing blows or drips onto fresh polish and coatings, which is hard to promise on a driveway.

  3. Indoors and secure while it cures

    Your car stays inside the studio while coatings cure, rather than sitting out on the street.

The process

From first message to handover.

  2. Consultation

    We walk you round the finished car under the same studio light.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need paint correction before a ceramic coating?

Usually, if the paint has swirls or scratches, because a coating seals in whatever is underneath it. We'll look at your paint under studio light and tell you honestly whether it needs correcting first.

How long will my car be with you?

It depends on the car's condition and the work you choose. We'll confirm it at the inspection, before anything is booked in.

Do you do mobile detailing?

No. We work from our studio, where we control the light and keep the car out of the wind, rain and dust.

How much will it cost?

Every car is priced after inspection, because condition varies so much from car to car. Use the enquiry builder above to tell us about yours.

Can you fix scratches and dents?

We do cosmetic repairs to scratches, dents and paint damage. Send us a few photos and we'll tell you what we think can be done.

Where is the studio?

Irthlingborough, near Wellingborough, Northamptonshire. We'll send you the exact location when we book you in.
