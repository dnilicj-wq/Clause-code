# Where the new site beats gafflowresinfinishes.com

These are plain-English points Dan can use when he writes to Gafflow.

1. **It loads in under 2 seconds instead of 8.** Lighthouse mobile on the current home page gives Performance 28, Accessibility 89, a largest paint of 8.4 seconds, 2.5 seconds of blocked main thread and a 2.4 MB page.

   | | Current site | New site (live demo) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Mobile performance (home) | 28 | 95 |
   | Accessibility | 89 | 100 |
   | Largest contentful paint | 8.4 s | 1.7 s |
   | Total blocking time | 2,510 ms | 250 ms |

   Inner pages score 97 to 100 with LCP 1.4 to 1.7 s. The launch build (search indexing on) scores 100 for SEO.

2. **A homeowner can design their floor before they call.** "Design your floor" is a live, poured metallic floor rendered in the browser. Visitors pick one of the four Gafflow base pigments and any of the 20 metallic pigments, and move across it to catch the light like a gloss floor. "Quote me this floor" carries the design into the quote form. Every colour links to that pigment in the shop, so the same moment sells installs and products.

3. **The award and the people lead, not the logo.** The National Flooring Awards 2025 win is in the first line of the home page and on every installation page, with Harry, Ollie and Lou named. Today it sits halfway down the page. Real testimonials, which are buried on their own page now, appear on the home page, the courses page and every course page.

4. **Every enquiry has a path.** Today the contact page has no form, and nothing gives a price. The new site has:
   - a 30-second estimator with indicative ranges, with the rates in one file Gafflow sets;
   - a 4-step quote form with photo upload and a postcode check;
   - WhatsApp on every page, and a sticky Call / WhatsApp / Quote bar on mobile.

   Courses show live dates with a "Starts in 16 days" countdown, so the past 29/30 October date and the out-of-date "January 2026" workshop line are gone.

5. **The three businesses work together.** "Every layer is a Gafflow product" takes a floor apart on scroll: primer, Big Base, Money Coat and seal, each linked to its product and price. Installations, courses and the shop are one site with one menu, and all 65 URLs and product slugs are kept, so no rankings are lost. The site adds course, product, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data and a working river-table resin calculator. It also fixes a broken link: the client contract link on the policies page currently sends people to the home page.
