Expert installations

Resin Driveways to suit your needs.

Resin driveways offer a modern and stylish alternative to traditional driveway surfaces, making them increasingly popular among homeowners. Composed of a mixture of resin and aggregate materials, resin driveways provide a smooth, seamless finish that enhances the curb appeal of any property. One of the standout features of resin driveways is their versatility in design.

With a wide range of colours, textures, and finishes available, homeowners have the freedom to create a driveway that perfectly complements their property's aesthetic. Whether opting for a sleek contemporary look or a more rustic feel, resin driveways can be tailored to suit individual preferences, adding a touch of sophistication to any home.

What are the benefits of resin driveways?

Composed of a blend of high-quality resin and aggregates, resin driveways are highly resistant to cracking, fading, and staining, ensuring a long-lasting surface that maintains its pristine appearance for years to come.

Resin driveways can be combined with decorative elements such as aggregates, glass beads, or even imprinted patterns, allowing for endless possibilities in design customisation. With their durability, versatility, and aesthetic appeal, resin driveways stand as a practical and stylish choice for enhancing the curb appeal and functionality of any home.

How do we lay resin driveways?

Step 1: Preparation and Excavation

Our team begins by clearing the area where the driveway will be installed, removing any vegetation, debris, or obstacles. Next, the existing surface is excavated to the required depth to accommodate the base layers. A stable and permeable base is crucial for the longevity and performance of the resin driveway. We then lay and compact a layer of porous aggregate material to create a solid foundation that promotes proper drainage and prevents water pooling.

Step 2: Sub-base and Base Layer

Once the base is installed and properly compacted, our team proceeds with the application of the resin-bound mixture. The resin and aggregates are carefully mixed on-site to achieve the desired colour, texture, and consistency. Our team then apply the resin-bound mixture evenly onto the prepared base using specialised equipment and techniques.

Step 3: Application

Our team meticulously smoothes and levels the resin-bound mixture, paying close attention to edges and transitions for a seamless finish. Once the surface is evenly distributed, we allow the resin to cure and harden, typically taking several hours to set fully. During this curing period, it's essential to protect the driveway from foot traffic and inclement weather to ensure optimal results.

How long does it take to install a resin driveway?

The duration of a resin driveway installation can vary depending on factors such as the size of the area, the complexity of the design, and weather conditions. On average, however, a typical resin driveway installation may take anywhere from a few days to a week to complete.

Are resin driveways suitable for all types of properties?

Yes, resin driveways are highly versatile and can be installed on various types of properties, including residential homes, commercial properties, and public spaces. They offer customisation options in terms of colour, texture, and design, making them suitable for a wide range of architectural styles and preferences.

How durable are resin driveways?

Resin driveways are known for their exceptional durability and longevity. Composed of a blend of high-quality resin and aggregates, resin driveways are highly resistant to cracking, fading, and staining, providing a long-lasting surface that withstands heavy foot traffic and vehicular use.

How do I maintain a resin driveway?

Maintenance for a resin driveway is relatively straightforward. Regular sweeping to remove debris and occasional pressure washing to clean the surface are typically sufficient. It's also advisable to refill any minor cracks or gaps with resin binder to maintain the integrity of the surface.
