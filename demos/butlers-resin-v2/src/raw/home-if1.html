About us

You can always
trust the butler

Butler's Resin & Construction is a Chatteris-based team laying resin bound driveways, patios and paths across Cambridgeshire.

Our vision is to become the leading installer of high-quality resin surfaces by giving every customer exceptional products and service. That standard is why The Resin Mill, the UK's largest resin bound products manufacturer, has recognised us as an Elite Installer.

  • Resin Mill Elite Installer
  • NRSWA qualified
  • 10+ years in the industry
  • Cambridgeshire & beyond

Why Butler's

Resin driveways engineered for strength & style

Every surface we lay starts with a properly prepared base, so it looks right on day one and for years after.

Fast installation

Most projects are completed in 4–6 days, and we confirm your timeline before we start.

Custom colours & designs

Pick your blend, then add borders, steps or patterns, right up to one-off inlays like our compass designs.

Durable & long-lasting

A properly installed resin bound surface can last 10–15 years or more with basic care.

No planning consent

Laid on an open-graded base, resin bound is fully permeable and SUDS compliant, which can avoid the need for planning permission.

Slip-resistant finish

A textured finish that's comfortable underfoot and keeps its grip, even in the wet.

Guaranteed workmanship

We assess every site first. If the base is weak, we dig out and rebuild rather than cover it up.
