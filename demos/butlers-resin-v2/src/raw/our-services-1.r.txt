BUTLER SERVICES

Driveways, groundwork and finishing. Done properly, first time.

❯
RESIN-BOUND DRIVEWAYS

We're passionate about delivering high-quality resin bound driveways that transform your property. Our team, with over a decade in the industry, crafts custom designs using Resin Mill products, ensuring durability and style. Kerb appeal comes as standard.

Resin Mill Elite Approved Installers
Custom colours & border designs
Permeable, low-maintenance finish
❮
❯
RESIN SURFACES

Resin isn't just for driveways. Yards, pathways, patios and surrounds get the same durable, permeable finish, laid wherever you need it. Smooth underfoot, easy to maintain, and built to handle British weather.

Yards, paths, patios & surrounds
Same Resin Mill quality throughout
Smooth, permeable, weed-free finish
❮
❯
BLOCK PAVING

Discover the charm of block paving with Butler's. We craft beautiful driveways, patios and outdoor spaces using top-quality materials, tailored to your preferences. Every project is completed to a high standard, adding value to your property.

Herringbone, basketweave & custom patterns
Proper sub-base preparation
Adds real value to your property
❮
❯
FENCING

Enhance your property's privacy and style with Butler's fencing. Our team crafts sturdy, attractive fences tailored to your needs, blending seamlessly with your outdoor space to create a secure and welcoming environment.

Closeboard, panel & sleeper options
Concrete or timber posts
Straight lines, solid footings
❮
❯
TILING

Whether it's a kitchen splashback, bathroom walls or a full wet room, Butler's delivers tiling that's built to last and finished to perfection. We work with porcelain, ceramic and natural stone, handling everything from surface prep to grouting.

Precise cuts, straight lines
Full surface preparation
Kitchens, bathrooms & wet rooms
❮
❯
FLOORING (INDOORS)

From luxury vinyl and laminate to engineered hardwood, Butler's can transform any room from the ground up. We handle full floor preparation, underlay and fitting, making sure every plank sits flush and every transition is clean.

LVT, laminate & engineered wood
Full prep & underlay included
Clean transitions, flush finish
❮
❯
PRESSURE WASHING

Refresh your outdoor areas with Butler's pressure washing. We expertly clean driveways, patios and more, restoring them to their original shine and preparing them for their next transformation.

Driveways, patios & paths
Restores colour & finish
Ideal prep before resealing
DROP KERBS

Need vehicle access to your new driveway? As NRSWA Qualified professionals, we handle drop kerb installations properly, from council approval to final finish, so your driveway is legal, accessible and done right.

NRSWA Qualified for highway work
Guidance through council approval
Tied in cleanly with your driveway
