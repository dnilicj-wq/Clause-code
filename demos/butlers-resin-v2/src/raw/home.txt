Frequently Asked Questions
Please reach us at
info@butlersrc.co.uk
if you cannot find an answer to your question.
What’s the difference between resin-bound and resin-bonded surfaces?
Resin-bound is permeable, smooth, and ideal for driveways. The stones are fully mixed with resin before being laid.
Resin-bonded is rougher, less durable, and uses resin as a base with loose stone scattered over it.
How long does a resin driveway or paved surface last?
A properly installed resin-bound surface can last 10 to 15 years or more with basic maintenance and care. Block paving and tarmac also have similar lifespans, depending on weather and use.
Is a resin driveway permeable/SUDS compliant?
Yes - resin-bound driveways are fully permeable if installed on a proper open-graded base. This can help avoid planning permission in some areas.
Can resin be laid over existing surfaces?
Sometimes. Resin can be installed over concrete or tarmac if the base is structurally sound. However, we always assess the condition first, a weak base means we recommend a full dig-out and rebuild.
Can you combine resin with other features?
Absolutely. We can integrate block edging, steps, ramps, fencing, garden borders, and even custom patterns to match your design vision.
How long does installation take?
Most standard domestic driveways take 4-6 days. Larger or more complex sites may take longer, but we always confirm timelines in advance.
Can I drive on my resin driveway straight after installation?
We recommend waiting 24 to 48 hours before walking or driving on your new resin surface. This ensures the resin fully cures and reaches its maximum strength.
Will weeds grow through my resin or paving surface?
No. Resin-bound surfaces are
sealed and compact
, so weeds can’t grow through. However, seeds landing on top can still sprout, so occasional sweeping or light weeding is advised.
