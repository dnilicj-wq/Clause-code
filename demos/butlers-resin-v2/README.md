# Butler's Resin & Construction: v2 demo

Live: https://clientgateway-butlers-resin-v2.dnilicj.workers.dev (Cloudflare Worker `clientgateway-butlers-resin-v2`, noindex while a demo).

Plain static HTML generated by `build/build.mjs` (no framework). The whole site is one Worker serving `dist/`, plus `POST /api/quote`, which saves leads and photos to the KV namespace `butlers-v2-quotes`.

```
npm i && node build/build.mjs                # writes dist/ (45 pages, WebP images)
cd <dir with wrangler.jsonc> && npx wrangler@4 deploy   # see wrangler.example.jsonc
```

## Sources (nothing invented)
- Copy, FAQ, the "Why Butler's" points, services, project pages, photos, compass video and logo all come from butlersrc.co.uk, including the iframe blocks. The raw pages are saved in `src/raw/`.
- Checkatrade: 10/10 from 18 reviews (read 2026-11-10). They are not a Checkatrade member. The 6 reviews Checkatrade shows are copied word for word and may be cut short where Checkatrade puts "Read more".
- Facebook: the 12 reviews from the review widget on their own site, copied word for word. Every review card in that widget links to their Facebook page.
- The blend swatch photos are the Resin Mill close-ups their project pages use.
- Area intros are ours and stick to geography plus their own projects. Distances are straight-line miles from Chatteris.
