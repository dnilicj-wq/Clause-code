Privacy

Who we are. COLT Building Contractors, 18 High Street, High Wycombe HP11 2BE. Email info@coltbuildingcontractors.co.uk.

What we collect. When you ask for a quote we collect the details you give us: the type of job, how urgent it is, your postcode, any photos, your name, mobile number and (if you add it) your email.

Why. Only to reply about your roof, arrange a free survey and prepare your quote. We don't sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time by emailing info@coltbuildingcontractors.co.uk.
