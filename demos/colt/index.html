    Questions, answered

    Do I have to pay a deposit?

    No. There's no deposit, and you only pay the balance once you're happy with the work. Credit cards are accepted.

    How long do I have to decide on a quote?

    Every quote is free, with no obligation, and comes with a 14-day cooling-off period.

    Who will actually be on my roof?

    Our own roofing team. We're family-run, three generations and over 25 years in roofing, and we don't use subcontractors.

    Will I be stuck with scaffolding for weeks?

    No. Scaffolding is done in-house by Colt Scaffolding and is free on every job. Under the Colt Promise, scaffolding and waste are removed within 24 hours of the job being completed.

    Is the work guaranteed?

    Yes. All work is fully guaranteed, and we carry £5 million public liability insurance.
