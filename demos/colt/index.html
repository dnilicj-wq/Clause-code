Anatomy of a Colt roof

What goes on above your ceiling, layer by layer. Scroll to build it.

  1. Rafters

    A structure set out true.

    New or repaired timbers come first. Roof structures and extensions are part of what we do, so the frame is right before anything goes on it.

  2. Membrane

    A breathable layer under everything.

    Laid over the rafters to keep wind-driven rain out of the loft while letting moisture escape.

  3. Battens

    Fixed to gauge.

    Battens set at the right spacing so every course of tiles or slates sits straight and fixes securely.

  4. Tiles or slates

    Laid by our own roofers.

    From Marley, Sandtoft, Forticrete and more, laid by our own roofing team. No subcontractors.

  5. Ridge

    Dry ridge and dry verge.

    Mechanically fixed systems at the ridge and verges, with no mortar to crack and wash out.

  6. Leadwork

    Chimneys sealed properly.

    Chimneys and leadwork done in-house, dressed where the roof meets the stack. Then the scaffold comes down within 24 hours.
