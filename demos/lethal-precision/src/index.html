Why most floors peel (and why ours don't)

Most coating failures come down to three things: skipped prep, the wrong product and a rushed install. Big-box DIY kits sit on top of the concrete instead of bonding to it, so hot tyres lift them right off.

Straight answers

Why are you more than the cheapest quote?

Because we grind, moisture-test, repair cracks and put down three coats. The cheapest quotes skip most of that, which is why those floors peel. Most double-garage floors we do land between {TypicalLow} and {TypicalHigh}.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, from {FinanceFrom}/month with approved credit through a finance partner. It's optional, and paying outright is always fine.

How long does it last?

With proper prep and a polyaspartic system, decades of normal use. That's why prep is the step we never skip.

Will hot tyres lift it?

Not with our system. Hot-tyre pickup is a classic failure of thin DIY epoxy, which is exactly what we don't use.

Is it slippery?

Flake finishes add texture. We can add extra grip for wet areas.

What about cracks in my concrete?

We repair them as part of the prep.

How long until I can use my garage?

Flake floors: walk on it the same evening, park after {CureTime}. Metallic floors take two to three days to install and a few more before cars go on.

Why not just buy a kit?

Kits skip the grinding and use thinner products. That's why most “I regret epoxy” stories start with a kit.
